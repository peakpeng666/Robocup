# K1 training restoration kit

This small optional asset restores the complete current training source/assets, scripts, configurations, environment locks, bootstrap, diagnostics and the iteration-70 candidate checkpoint. Existing licensed Isaac Gym/Python/CUDA dependencies remain local. Full competition binaries, videos, other checkpoints and complete history remain in the full snapshot archives.

The candidate is not approved for competition or robot deployment. No training is started by restoring files.

This product includes software developed by B-Human (http://www.b-human.de). Third-party licenses remain with their source directories.
